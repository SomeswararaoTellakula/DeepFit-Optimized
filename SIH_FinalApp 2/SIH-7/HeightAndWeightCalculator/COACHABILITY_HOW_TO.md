# Coachability Challenge: how to take it

The Coachability Challenge does not measure strength. It measures **how fast you learn**.

- DeepFit shows you a new pattern of 5 moves.
- You do the pattern **5 times**.
- After each try you get **one tip** about your biggest mistake.
- If your mistakes drop quickly from try 1 to try 5, you get a high score.

It takes about 3–4 minutes.

---

## 1. Before you start

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Camera | Laptop or phone camera at chest height, **facing you** (not side-on) |
| Distance | About **2.5–3 m** from the camera. Your **whole body, head to feet**, must be in the picture |
| Space | Room to take **one step left and one step right**, and to jump |
| Light | Light in front of you, not behind you (no window behind you) |
| People | Only you in the picture |
| Clothes | Clothes that show your arms and knees clearly (not a loose shawl or long coat) |

## 2. Open the test

1. Log in.
2. Open **Coachability** in the top menu, or open the **Tests** hub and choose **Coachability Challenge**.
   - The direct link is `/ex/coachability`.
3. Pick the mode: **Standard**, **Blind** (everything is spoken) or **Deaf** (big cards and captions).
4. Press **Verify Identity** and look straight at the camera until it says verified.
5. Press **Start**.

## 3. The 11 moves the camera knows

Each step of the pattern is one of these moves:

| Move | What to do | Tip so the camera sees it |
|---|---|---|
| Both hands up | Both arms straight up above your head | Hands clearly above your head |
| Left hand up | Only your **left** hand up, right arm down | "Left" means **your own** left |
| Right hand up | Only your **right** hand up, left arm down | "Right" means **your own** right |
| Hands on head | Both hands on top of your head | Elbows out to the sides |
| Arms out to the sides | Arms straight out at shoulder height, like a "T" | Keep them level with your shoulders |
| Hands on hips | Both hands on your hips | **Bend your elbows**; hands just hanging by your hips do not count |
| Squat down | Bend your knees, like sitting on a chair | Go down clearly, then stand up |
| Touch your knees | Bend forward and put both hands on your knees | Both hands on the knees |
| Step to your left | One clear step to **your** left | A full step, not a lean |
| Step to your right | One clear step to **your** right | A full step, not a lean |
| Jump | A small jump with both feet | Both feet leave the floor |

**Hold each move for about half a second.** The camera needs about 1/3 of a second of the same pose to count it.

**Mirror warning:** the screen usually shows you like a mirror. Always use **your own** left and right, not the left or right of the screen.

## 4. One try, step by step

1. **Get ready.** Stand still facing the camera with your **arms down by your sides**.
   - The try starts only after you have stood like this for about 1 second.
2. **Learn.** The 5-move pattern is shown as numbered cards and spoken aloud, for example:
   `1 Hands on head · 2 Step to your left · 3 Squat down · 4 Right hand up · 5 Jump`.
   You have a few seconds to remember it.
3. **Go!** Do the 5 moves **in order**.
   - After each move, come back towards arms-down before the next one.
   - The screen shows each move the camera saw: a tick if it was right, a cross if it was wrong.
4. **Finish.** When you have done the 5 moves, stand still with your arms down.
   - After about 2 seconds of standing still, the try ends by itself.
5. **Tip.** You see and hear your number of mistakes and **one tip**, for example "Step 3 is squat down – you did touch your knees".
   - Read it, then get ready for the next try.

The pattern is the **same in all 5 tries**. That is the point: we see how quickly you learn it.

Each new attempt of the challenge gets a **new** pattern, with a code such as `#L88DF`, so nobody can practise it beforehand.

## 5. How mistakes are counted

| Mistake | Counts as |
|---|---|
| Wrong move at a step | 1 error |
| Missed move | 1 error |
| Extra move that is not in the pattern | 1 error |
| A move that starts more than 2.5 s after the previous one | 0.5 error (late) |

## 6. How the score is made (0–100)

- **65%** of the score comes from **how much your errors drop** from try 1 to the average of tries 4 and 5.
- **35%** comes from **how accurate you are at the end**.
- If try 1 is already perfect, learning can't be measured. The score is then capped at 90, with a note.
- The percentile uses a starter reference (average 45) until DeepFit has real data from many athletes.

Example:

| Try | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Errors | 4 | 3 | 1.5 | 0.5 | 0 |

The errors fall quickly and the last tries are almost perfect, so this athlete gets a **high** coachability score.

The score is also used in the **Hidden Potential Index**, where coachability is 40% of the total.

## 7. Blind and deaf modes

- **Blind mode:**
  - Every step of the pattern is spoken with its number before each try.
  - A tone confirms every move the camera recognises.
  - The tip is spoken.
  - Left and right are always your own body's left and right, so you don't need to see the screen.
- **Deaf mode:**
  - The pattern is shown as numbered cards, and the current step lights up.
  - Green and red flashes show right and wrong moves.
  - The tip is shown in large captions.

## 8. If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| It never says "Go" | Stand still with your arms fully down, facing the camera, whole body in view |
| Moves are not recognised | Step back so your feet and hands are in the picture; hold each move longer; improve the light |
| Left and right come out swapped | Use **your own** left and right, not the screen's |
| Hands on hips is not counted | Bend your elbows and put your hands **on** your hips |
| The try ends too early | Don't stand still with your arms down in the middle of the pattern; go straight to the next move |
| "Face does not match" | Face the camera, and keep only the registered athlete in the picture |

## 9. Quick demo for the review (1 minute)

1. Open **Coachability**, choose **Standard**, press **Verify Identity**, then **Start**.
2. Read the pattern aloud as it appears.
3. In **try 1**, make one mistake on purpose, for example a wrong move at step 3. Show the tip it gives.
4. In **try 2**, do the pattern correctly. Point out that the errors went down. That drop is exactly what the score measures.
