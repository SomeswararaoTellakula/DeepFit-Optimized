How to check it yourself (10 minutes)

  1. One person does the test; a second person watches and counts by hand (or times with a stopwatch, or measures with a tape).
  2. Write down both numbers - the app's result and the hand value - and compare them.
  3. Repeat with 8-10 different people and different cameras. (On the phone app the hand value can be typed under the result, and then it appears in the table above.)
