📏 Height - measured by the camera

  1. Print the two DeepFit sheets (FLOOR + TOP, at 100 %) - or use two plain A4 sheets on a darker wall.
  2. FLOOR sheet: cut on the dashed line, stand it on the floor against the wall, tape it flat.
  3. TOP sheet: tape it on the same wall anywhere above your head, a bit to the side - no measuring.
  4. Stand next to the floor sheet: no shoes or cap, heels, back and head on the wall, hair flat.
  5. Camera 2-3 m away at chest height - both sheets, your head and your feet in view.

Why two sheets: one sheet can't tell how much the camera is tilted (2° of tilt = 4 cm wrong). With both, tilt is measured and removed. Expected accuracy ≈ ±1 cm.
